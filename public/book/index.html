    Stocktake

    Type the actual number on the shelf, or tap ✓ All here if it matches. Two teachers can count different shelves at the same time.

    Teacher only

    Teacher view

    Borrow requests, current loans, returns and records. Nothing goes on loan, and nothing counts as returned, until a teacher confirms it here.

    Signed in as teacher

    Borrow requests

    Students' requests and pre-orders. Nothing is on loan until you tap Confirm and hand over. Pre-orders show the day they'll collect, so you can pack them ahead.

    Current loans and returns

    Everyone who has gear out, one row per student. Tap a name to see their items. Students who've handed something back show to check at the top. Check it's all there, then confirm.

    You're signed in with a student account, so you can see loans here but can't confirm returns or change the inventory. Open this page on your own device, signed in to your teacher account, to do that.

    Teachers

    Only people on this list get the Teacher tab without a password and can mark returns in one step. Students never become teachers, whatever access they're given.

    Loan history

    One row per student, most recent first. Tap a name to see all of their loans and what was borrowed. Tick a loan (or single items) and tap Mark not returned to put them back on loan.

    QR stickers

    Print stickers and put one on each piece of gear. Then, on Return gear, tap a student's "Scan returns" and hold each item up to the camera.

    Backup and records

    Everything is saved online and updates live on every device. Download a backup each term for safekeeping.

    Records that disappeared

    If a loan is removed without you confirming a return, it shows up here so nothing goes missing quietly.

    Inventory

    Your full equipment list. Change names, notes, categories or quantities and they save straight away. The picture comes from the category. Untick "Can borrow" to keep an item in the stocktake but hide it from students. Cameras, handycams and GoPros are numbered 1 to the quantity.
